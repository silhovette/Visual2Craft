[COORDINATE SYSTEM AND DIMENSION CONSISTENCY]

The building front is the entrance/featured facade where supported; otherwise choose the clearest stable facade, and remain consistent.
All scene coordinates and `base_y` are NONNEGATIVE integers. The scene size is [width,height,depth], with maximum dimensions 64 x 48 x 64 and total volume within 200000 cells. `base_y` must lie within the scene height. Every 'from'/'to' pair is inclusive, has exactly three integer coordinates, and lies inside size. Do not invent large padding areas, unrelated terrain or unseen backyards. Ensure every roof, eave, stair step, window, inner void and off-axis detail occupies a plausible and consistent coordinate.
Keep one scale across the whole scene: 1 vanilla full block = 1 grid cell. Model slabs/stairs/trapdoors using their real block states, not arbitrary fractional coordinates. Reassess proportions after assigning coordinates; do not silently stretch one facade relative to another. Orient 'facing' and other directional states in world-coordinate terms, not camera-relative words.

VISUAL2CRAFT — BLOCK SEMANTICS & SPECIAL-BLOCK POLICY v2.0

[ORDINARY BLOCK SEMANTICS]
Determine ordinary block identity from chronological placement/removal, shape, thickness, cross-view geometry, texture, held items and readable labels where useful; shader color or brightness alone is not decisive. Brightness alone is NOT evidence for an invisible Light block. Distinguish solid glass vs connected panes, full blocks vs slabs/stairs, water vs transparent solids, waterlogged host blocks vs standalone water, and air vs any real block. Visible lamps/torches/lanterns/glowstone may be inferred from normal visual evidence; they are NOT minecraft:light.

[SHELF VS TRAPDOOR]
Treat a shelf/display shelf and a trapdoor as different block families even when both use wood textures. A shelf such as minecraft:oak_shelf or minecraft:bamboo_shelf is a thick horizontal storage/display block: it forms a continuous ledge or cubby, occupies the shelf's solid depth, and supports/display items on its top or face. A trapdoor is a thin hinged panel with a visible hinge/opening action or a panel-like closed face; its valid states use facing, half, open, powered and possibly waterlogged. Do not infer a trapdoor from a flat horizontal shelf surface, and do not infer a shelf from a thin hinged panel or a door-like opening. Use chronological placement, thickness, support geometry, hinge/open state, item placement and readable names together. If those cues do not distinguish them, emit a local unknown operation rather than substituting one for the other.

air = deliberate clearing only when confirmed; invisible or occluded space does NOT imply air. structure_void is a real block only when supported as an actual placement; action=preserve means issue NO command, not place structure_void. water[level=0] is a source; flowing water may have other valid levels. Lava and magma materials are prohibited in this project: replace visible lava/magma-like regions with a similar orange/yellow luminous solid such as ochre froglight or glowstone. One cell cannot contain separate water and a waterlogged host simultaneously.

[TRANSPARENT MATERIAL VS INVISIBLE BARRIER — DO NOT CONFUSE]
Glass and glass panes are ordinary visible Minecraft blocks. Choose minecraft:glass or a stained-glass/pane ID only when the supplied frames show their visible edges, thickness, texture, reflections, connected pane geometry, or a direct placement/material cue. A transparent surface is still a real visible block; it is not a Barrier merely because the background can be seen through it.
Barrier is an invisible full-collision special block with no visible texture. Choose minecraft:barrier ONLY under the strict readable video-text rule below: the supplied frame text must explicitly instruct placement of a Barrier block (or the exact localized equivalent) and identify its exact or unambiguous grid position. Visual transparency, apparent collision, water containment, an empty-looking wall, missing texture, shadows, brightness, or a user hint is never evidence for Barrier.
If a region appears transparent but no glass material is visually supported and no explicit Barrier placement text exists, use the most local unknown/preserve representation supported by the evidence; never invent Barrier. If visible glass evidence exists, place glass/panes even when they are transparent. Barrier and glass must never be treated as interchangeable substitutes.

[LIGHT AND BARRIER — STRICT VIDEO-TEXT-ONLY ACTIVATION]
Only place minecraft:light or minecraft:barrier when READABLE TEXT PRESENT IN THE SUPPLIED VIDEO FRAME(S), such as subtitles or an on-screen building instruction, EXPRESSLY TELLS THE BUILDER TO PLACE THAT SPECIFIC BLOCK and provides an exact coordinate or unambiguous relative position that maps to the common 3D grid. For Barrier, qualifying wording may be "Barrier", "barrier block", "invisible barrier", "transparent barrier", "transparent block", or an equivalent localized phrase such as "透明屏障" or "透明方块", but the surrounding text must clearly mean an invisible full-collision block rather than visible glass. Both block identity and location must be unambiguous.

Never activate from visual brightness, shadows, absence of lamps, invisible walls, collision/movement restrictions, held items, hotbar icons, audio alone, general discussion/mention of the block, or USER BUILDING HINTS. Unreadable, truncated, merely descriptive, contradictory, or location-less text is insufficient. Do not substitute these blocks for air, glass or ordinary walls.

minecraft:light is invisible and non-collidable but real. Emit minecraft:light[level=N], N in 0..15. Use the exact level stated in the on-video placement instruction; if it qualifies but states no level, use 15. Never infer level from ambient brightness. Do not invent waterlogged=true.
minecraft:barrier is invisible and full-collision but real. Emit minecraft:barrier at the instructed position; never infer it from apparent collisions.

[TRACEABLE FINAL BLUEPRINT — CRITICAL]
For EACH confirmed Light/Barrier placement, create a REAL action=place, confidence=confirmed operation with the exact block ID/states and mapped from/to coordinates. evidence.frames must include the actual provided video frame ID containing the readable instruction. evidence.observation must quote that video text accurately (in its original language when possible) and state its coordinate mapping.
